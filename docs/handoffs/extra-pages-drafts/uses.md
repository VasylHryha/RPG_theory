## Begin with what engineers can already control

A vibrating plate can move particles. An acoustic field can hold and move microscopic objects. Arranging a material differently can change its larger-scale behaviour. These give concrete starting points for asking how activity, shape and surroundings affect one another.

**RRG's application hypothesis** is that understanding this feedback might help design organizations that maintain themselves, spread through compatible material and create useful conditions for further organization. The three groups below distinguish existing engineering, RRG-inspired trials and distant possibilities.

## Already done by others with similar principles

**Materials and metamaterials.** Ordinary engineering uses known behaviour to build a device. A metamaterial gets chosen larger-scale properties from its arrangement. The sources also discuss repeated driving that changes how a system behaves under chosen conditions, with a [controlled optical-lattice experiment](/evidence/catalogue/#e15--realization-of-the-hofstadter-hamiltonian-with-ultracold-atoms-in-optical-lattices) as one example. This changes behaviour under particular conditions while fundamental laws still apply. These are the first engineering levels in the [source discussion](/framework/recursive-background/#31-engineering-consequence-from-using-laws-to-designing-effective-laws).

**Manipulation through vibration and sound.** The [Chladni study](/evidence/catalogue/#e02--controlling-the-motion-of-multiple-objects-on-a-chladni-plate) uses controlled plate excitation to move particles; the particles contact the plate. The [acoustic-tweezers study](/evidence/catalogue/#e03--independent-trapping-and-manipulation-of-microparticles-using-dexterous-acoustic-tweezers) traps and manipulates microscopic particles with an engineered ultrasonic array in liquid, without a mechanical gripper. Both require apparatus, driving and pre-existing objects.

**Chemical cooperation.** In the [RNA-network experiment](/evidence/catalogue/#e13--spontaneous-network-formation-among-cooperative-rna-replicators), selected RNA fragments formed catalytic networks: networks of reactions whose products help other reactions proceed. Tested cooperative networks had growth advantages over competing cycles. Selected sequences and controlled chemical conditions were supplied. These independently developed examples demonstrate limited mechanisms, not a shared RRG mechanism across all scales.

## What RRG suggests trying

**Materials:** measure both directions of the feedback. Does selected driving maintain an arrangement, and does that arrangement then change the supported activity? Compare the result with existing material models.

**Sound:** move beyond a prescribed pattern to ask whether the resulting geometry contributes to a measurable change in the surrounding field. That would investigate the gap between controlled manipulation and organization changing its [background](/glossary/#background), the surrounding material, activity and conditions. The apparatus and external control must remain part of the account.

**Microbes and environment:** study persistence, environmental change and spread as separate observations. The plain explanation uses life changing local conditions as a visible analogy. A proposed microbial system would need to show which changed conditions help further organization and which standard biological mechanisms already explain them.

**AI:** try small dynamic modules with adaptive relations and several timescales, allowing stable clusters to become higher-level units. This follows the [AI proposal](/documents/world-explanation/#13-ai-may-be-using-an-indirect-route). Compare learning or prediction against an appropriate baseline; stable activity alone is not intelligence.

These directions move from controlling shape and activity toward the harder proposal that an organization can recruit compatible surroundings. No RRG-specific engineering or learning benefit has been established.

## Far-future speculation: self-maintaining habitats

Imagine a habitat in which local organization helps maintain useful conditions and those conditions support further organization. This is a speculative application of the sources' strongest engineering vision, not an available design or a reported result. It would require demonstrated control, compatible material and continuing resources.

The farthest engineering level considers deliberately choosing how things behave locally through controlled shape and activity. The sources call this “magic” as a metaphor for advanced engineering, guided by the proposed [resonance-fit](/glossary/#resonance-fit): forces hold things together while the fit of shape and activity would decide which arrangements last. They do not establish arbitrary rule selection, and changing a frequency is not a universal instruction for building anything.
