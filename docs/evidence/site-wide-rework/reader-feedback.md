# Whole-site formative reader protocol

Status: **NOT_PERFORMED**. There are no recruited participants, interviews,
transcripts, ratings or real assistive-technology observations. The author's
reported difficulty prompted the rework; it is distinct from an unfamiliar-reader
study. The checks and screenshots in verification.json are engineering evidence.

Use a small mix of newcomers, science-literate and technical readers when
available. Do not send outreach without an authorized audience/channel. Record
consent, candidate commit plus dirty-input/artifact identity, actual device and
pages seen. Keep identities and recordings private; share only consented anonymous
findings. Show the candidate, not the unchanged live edition.

Say: “We are testing the explanation, not you. Agreement is not expected.” Observe
before explaining or hinting at answers. Use the following neutral tasks:

| Journey | Task |
|---|---|
| Home → Start → example | Describe the proposed idea in your own words. Show what happened in the example and how you think it connects. |
| Concept → exact definition | Find what this term means here. Explain it, then show where the formal meaning is recorded. |
| Evidence → case → cited source | Choose a finding. What did the study report? Which part is the project's interpretation? Find the original source. |
| Research → open question → contribution | Find something the project has not established. What could someone contribute, and where would they send it? |
| Technical reading → mathematics → limits | Find the assumptions and the result. What does the argument depend on, and where does its scope stop? |
| Documents → download → citation/history | Obtain the intended document and identify the edition you would cite. Was the download an original source or an explanatory export? |

After each task, ask which passage was hard to picture, where the reader paused,
what question the next link should answer and why they would continue or stop.
Record observed navigation separately from the participant's explanation.

Classify misunderstanding, attribution error, navigation trouble, accessibility
obstacle, lack of interest and scientific disagreement separately. Accurate
understanding with an objection to novelty is not a comprehension failure;
enjoyment without understanding is not a comprehension success. Apply repeated
findings across relevant page families, retaining the exact pages actually seen.
No population-wide or peer-review claim follows from these formative observations.

Use reader-feedback-template.json privately for actual observations. Empty fields
mean no study. Never fill them with simulated people or inferred answers. Real
screen-reader and other AT observations must identify the actual tool and task.
Unavailable participants do not halt implementation or manufacture approval.
