# Candidate A: reader-feedback protocol

Status: NOT_PERFORMED. Candidate A was selected editorially from the owner-provided
review. Author feedback about unclear writing is distinct from independent readers,
general communication guidance, automated checks and source-fidelity acceptance.

Invite unfamiliar readers with different backgrounds when available. Do not contact
people automatically. Obtain consent before recording. Keep identifying information
and recordings private; publish only consented anonymous findings.

Record the exact candidate commit, artifact identity and pages each person actually
saw. Use the new candidate preview, not the unchanged live edition. Before showing
Home and Start, do not explain RRG verbally or signal an expected positive answer.
Say: “We are testing the explanation, not you, and agreement is not expected.”

Ask these open questions before probing specific concepts:

1. Describe in your own words what the author is proposing.
2. Point to the example: what changed, and why does it matter here?
3. What is observed, proposed or uncertain?
4. Where would you go next, or why would you stop?
5. Which sentence needed rereading or was hard to picture?

Then explore geometry/activity, the two roles of a whole, recursion and the origin
hypothesis without suggesting the answers. Separate inability to follow the
explanation from informed disagreement about novelty, support or usefulness.
An accurate “this resembles emergence” response is not automatically a copy defect.
Keep objections as objections; do not edit participants into agreement.

Log actual observations using reader-feedback-template.json. Empty fields and an
empty response array mean no study was performed. Do not invent transcripts or
select a winner using simulated personas. A star-first opening remains an optional
future comparable candidate; it is not implemented or reader-tested here.

Real screen-reader checks remain NOT_PERFORMED. Automated accessibility and keyboard
checks, when recorded, are separate evidence. R4 remains the only forward tracker.
