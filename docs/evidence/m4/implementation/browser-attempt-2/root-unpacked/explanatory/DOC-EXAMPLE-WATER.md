## What to notice

A wave crest can travel across water as the water moves locally. Following the crest and following a small region of water give different descriptions. Ordinary waves transfer energy through their medium; [NOAA's wave explanation](../bibliography.md#bib-0060) explains this background physics.

## What this illustrates

The visible pattern belongs to the collective behaviour. This gives us a first way to ask about parts, relationships and activity together, before trying to identify a persistent object.

A passing ripple is a warm-up, not proof that a persistent higher-level unit has formed. The next example, a [string with fixed ends](./DOC-EXAMPLE-STRING.md), makes the role of constraints clearer.

## Ask the next question

Which physical conditions support the pattern? How long does it last? Those questions matter when we move from noticing a collective pattern to investigating the source's [meaning of stability](./DOC-CONCEPT-STABILITY.md).
