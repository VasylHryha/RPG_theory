## Conceptually locked

1. $R=(G,M)$: resonant geometry is geometry + full temporal mode structure.
2. $G\leftrightarrow M$: geometry supports/constrains mode structure; mode structure supports/maintains/transforms geometry.
3. Stability is that self-consistent closure for however long it exists; it is not permanence.
4. Higher structures may contain identical or different lower structures.
5. Lower-level dynamics remain active inside higher levels.
6. Different arrangements/compositions can create different higher resonant geometries.
7. Each scale may use different microscopic mechanisms and equations.
8. Replication is one possible mechanism, not a universal mandatory step.

## Evidence update: emergent interaction channels

Additional physics evidence supports the mechanism that a lower-level organized medium can generate a higher-level effective interaction:

- phonons mediating effective electron attraction in superconductivity;
- spin-ice geometry producing emergent magnetic charges with Coulomb-like interactions;
- the Higgs-field background changing the observable electroweak interaction regime.

This strengthens the downstream extension:


$$
R_n\rightarrow\mathcal I_{n+1}^{\rm eff}\rightarrow R_{n+1}.
$$


It does **not** close the claim that the four fundamental forces themselves are all deeper RRG manifestations.
