## 3. Background-field state changes the observable interaction regime

Electromagnetic and weak interactions are described within electroweak theory.

As the early Universe cooled, the Higgs field settled into a non-zero vacuum configuration. The $W$ and $Z$ bosons acquired mass while the photon remained massless.

That changes low-energy interaction behavior:

- massive $W/Z$ carriers imply a very short-range weak interaction;
- the massless photon supports long-range electromagnetism.
