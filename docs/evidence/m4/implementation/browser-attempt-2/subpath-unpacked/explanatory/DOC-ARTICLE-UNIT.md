## Explanation: a useful description depends on the question

A molecule contains atoms with internal activity, yet a description at another scale can treat the molecule as a participant in a reaction or as a rotating object. That useful simplification does not erase the atoms. It selects the collective properties relevant to an interaction. The supplied [world explanation](./DOC-CONCEPT-GEOMETRY.md) discusses this packaging of complexity, and the [molecule example](./DOC-EXAMPLE-MOLECULE.md) illustrates how collective constraints coexist with active parts.

A whole is not automatically a useful unit just because we can draw a boundary around it. The question is whether the organization persists in the circumstances of interest and supports a collective response that the next description can use. A description useful for one timescale or perturbation can be inadequate for another. When a molecule dissociates, continuing to treat it as the same intact participant would hide a material change.

RRG's geometry includes relations, connectivity, boundaries and constraints, as well as arrangement. Its mode structure includes phase, amplitude, coupling, propagation and characteristic times. One frequency number cannot stand in for all of that organization. The [string example](./DOC-EXAMPLE-STRING.md) makes the limitation concrete: supported patterns depend on boundaries, tension and mass per unit length. A standing-wave analogy is not itself proof of a self-maintaining higher-level object.

## Proposal: collective organization becomes reusable

The registered resonant geometry is [UT-D03](./UT-D03.md). The locked stability meaning, [UT-D04](./UT-D04.md), concerns a self-consistent geometry–mode organization. It does not mean permanence. Lifetimes and perturbation recovery are system-specific measurements of this meaning, rather than replacement definitions.

A collective organization can function as an effective unit in further interactions. Mechanisms may differ across scales.

This scale definition anchors the useful-unit question. In higher-level formation ([UT-D06](./UT-D06.md)), parts can be different, retain internal activity and play different roles. Their collective organization can then participate in another combination. The structural recursion ([UT-D08](./UT-D08.md)) does not require identical microscopic mechanisms at every step or a perfectly regular ladder of sizes and times.

To assess a candidate whole, a model needs to specify what counts as its organization, which interactions are relevant and when the collective description stops working. The [mathematical programme](./DOC-MATH.md) proposes tests and strong failure conditions. This article does not turn those proposed tests into a general theorem. The [stability concept](./DOC-CONCEPT-STABILITY.md) offers a shorter reading of the same boundary.

## Actual results: a description is not an execution receipt

No new result is derived or reproduced here. Familiar molecules and cells are explanatory examples, while the original documents report particular external and project evidence with their own scope. [Research status](./DOC-STATUS.md) and [the evidence index](./DOC-EVIDENCE.md) distinguish those reports from conjectures and unperformed work.

Treating a cell as a unit in an organism does not imply that all cells or all organisms follow a proved universal equation. Likewise, reported effective interactions do not establish unification of fundamental forces. Whether the organizing principle can be made quantitatively predictive across systems remains open.

A useful next question is concrete: for this interaction and interval, which collective properties suffice, and what observation would force us to describe the parts again?
