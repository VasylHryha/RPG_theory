## 1. Geometry $G$

In RRG, **geometry** means the organization of a system at a chosen scale:


$$
G=\{\text{components, arrangement, relations, connectivity, order, orientation, boundaries, constraints}\}.
$$


Geometry is not limited to visible Euclidean shape.

## 2. Frequency / mode structure $M$

In RRG, **frequency** is shorthand for the system's full temporal mode organization, not one scalar value in hertz:


$$
M=\{\omega,\phi,A,\psi,\text{propagation, coupling, characteristic timescales, response}\}.
$$


A scalar frequency alone is never assumed to uniquely determine a geometry.

## 3. Resonant geometry $R$

The basic RRG object is:


$$
\boxed{R=(G,M)}.
$$


Geometry and mode structure are inseparable aspects of one organized system:


$$
\boxed{G\leftrightarrow M}.
$$


- Geometry constrains/supports which modes can exist.
- The active mode structure supports, maintains, transforms, or destroys the geometry.

Neither side is defined as universally "first".

## 4. Stability

**Locked definition:** a geometry is stable in RRG while its geometry and mode structure form a self-consistent closed organization:


$$
\boxed{G\leftrightarrow M}.
$$


Stability is **not permanence**. A state may satisfy the closure for a very short or very long interval.

Lifetime, perturbation recovery, Lyapunov stability, decay width, or metastability are **measurements/tests of a particular system**, not replacements for this core definition.

## 5. Higher-level formation

A higher-level resonant geometry may be formed from any compatible collection of lower-level resonant geometries:


$$
\boxed{\{R_n^1,R_n^2,\ldots,R_n^m\}\rightarrow R_{n+1}}.
$$


The lower-level members:

- do not need to be identical;
- do not need to duplicate first;
- can have different roles and internal modes;
- remain internally active after the higher-level structure forms.

The higher level introduces new collective constraints and a new collective mode structure while containing the lower levels.

## 6. Variation

Different counts, arrangements, compositions, orientations, connections, phases, and environments may create different higher-level resonant geometries from the same lower-level building blocks.


$$
\boxed{\text{same or related lower units}+\text{different organization}\rightarrow\text{different }R_{n+1}}.
$$


Variation is therefore fully compatible with the core and requires no new principle.

## 7. Scale

A **scale** is a level at which a collective resonant geometry can function as an effective unit in further interactions.

Different scales may have different:

- characteristic sizes,
- characteristic times/frequencies,
- dominant interactions,
- effective mechanisms,
- compositions and geometries.

RRG does **not** require the same microscopic mechanism or identical equation at every scale.

## 8. Recursion

The core recursive claim is structural:


$$
\boxed{\{R_n^i\}\rightarrow R_{n+1}\rightarrow R_{n+2}\rightarrow\cdots}.
$$


At every level the locked relation remains:


$$
\boxed{R_n=(G_n,M_n),\qquad G_n\leftrightarrow M_n}.
$$


The mechanisms implementing the relation may change with scale.

## 9. Replication / recurrence

Replication, duplication, autocatalysis, reproduction, aggregation, differentiation, self-assembly, and symmetry breaking are **possible mechanisms of higher-level formation or propagation**, not mandatory steps in the core recursion.

RRG never requires:


$$
A\rightarrow A+A\rightarrow B
$$


at every level.

Biological replication is therefore an example, not the definition of recursion.

## 10. Interaction / force status

The following is **NOT locked as established fact**:

> known forces are identical to resonant frequencies or correspond one-to-one with scales.

The locked core only says that interactions occur within and between resonant geometries and can participate in forming higher-level geometries.

The stronger claim that gravity, electromagnetism, weak, and strong interactions are scale-dependent manifestations of deeper resonant geometry is an **RRG extension to be proven**, not a definition.

## 11. Background / possibility-space status

The idea that global cosmological/environmental conditions change which resonant geometries are accessible is an **extension compatible with the core**, not part of the minimal definition.

## 12. What counts as contradiction

An example does **not** contradict RRG merely because:

- the components are different;
- the higher level does not reproduce itself;
- the structure is short-lived;
- the mechanism differs from another scale;
- the system is stationary rather than visibly oscillating;
- lower-level units differentiate into different functions;
- the higher-level interaction differs from the lower-level interaction.

A genuine contradiction must show that a claimed RRG object cannot consistently be represented by the locked relation $R=(G,M)$, or that the proposed recursive higher-level organization cannot be expressed without violating one of the locked definitions.
