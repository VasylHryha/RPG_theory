## Explanation: conditions belong in the story

A pile of ingredients does not explain why a new organization forms. Arrangement, energy flow and surroundings matter. The supplied world explanation uses a star: a gravitationally organized body establishes pressure, temperature and reaction conditions that were unavailable to the earlier diffuse gas. The later availability of heavier elements expands the kinds of chemistry that can occur. This is the source's explanatory example, not a calculation performed for this article. See [the world explanation](./DOC-CONCEPT-GEOMETRY.md) and [the star example](./DOC-EXAMPLE-STAR.md).

The useful distinction is between having components and being able to combine them under particular conditions. A structure may alter those conditions. It can supply boundaries, concentrate material or maintain an environment in which a later structure can persist. The world explanation's retained background addendum describes this as a changing landscape of reachable possibilities. A possible configuration need not already exist as a finished object awaiting discovery.

The same reasoning helps explain why the surroundings cannot be removed from the story of life. Suitable chemistry and a maintained environment are enabling conditions; they do not guarantee the historical path to a cell. [Life and its environment](./DOC-EXAMPLE-LIFE.md) keeps that unresolved route visible. These examples use different mechanisms. Their similarity is an explanatory pattern, not evidence that every scale shares an identical equation.

## Proposal: recursive formation and changed surroundings

The locked core permits compatible, internally active lower-level units to form a collective organization. The units can differ and need not copy themselves first. The resulting whole introduces collective constraints and modes while its parts remain active. The registered meanings are [UT-D06](./UT-D06.md) and [UT-D08](./UT-D08.md); this article adds no definition.

RRG asks whether persistent organization can serve both as a reusable unit and as a contributor to the environment for further organization. That question is stronger than observing familiar cases. To test it, a particular model must say which conditions change, which new formations become reachable and what would count as failure. [The framework](./DOC-FRAMEWORK.md) and [the mathematical programme](./DOC-MATH.md) describe candidate ways to make such questions precise. They remain a research programme.

The effective-interaction extension adds another conditional possibility: lower-level organization may support an effective interaction channel relevant to a higher organization. The [interaction evidence](./DOC-INTERACTION-EVIDENCE.md) reports distinct physical cases. It does not establish fundamental-force unification or a universal quantitative law. The conjecture [UT-C01](./UT-C01.md) keeps that distinction explicit.
