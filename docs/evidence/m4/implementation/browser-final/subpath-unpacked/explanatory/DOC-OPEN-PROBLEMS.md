## What would count against an extension?

Read the [current proof matrix](./DOC-PROOF.md) for each claim’s reported status, evidence target, contrary result and core impact. The [framework’s failure criteria](./DOC-FRAMEWORK.md#15-what-would-falsify-or-weaken-specific-rrg-claims) distinguish core contradiction candidates from failed extensions: lack of a transferable invariant, predictive reduction, spontaneous hierarchy or novel prediction can weaken a proposed extension. Wrong known physics or trivial universality are also stated failure criteria.

The [mathematical model’s strong failure conditions](./DOC-MATH.md#19-strong-failure-conditions) include manually imposed clusters or hierarchy, lost predictive power after coarse-graining, and lack of robust persistence under perturbation. These are tests of that proposed implementation. [Metrics](./DOC-MATH.md#18-metrics) and [the next concrete calculation](./DOC-MATH.md#40-next-concrete-calculation) state the current programme; no calculation is reported as completed here.

## Effective interactions: the narrower open question

The [07 addendum’s final section](./DOC-INTERACTION-EVIDENCE.md#5-what-remains-open) asks under what general conditions an effective interaction generated or activated by one organization can stabilize or enable another. Its phonon, spin-ice and background-field examples do not close universal force unification. The [08 register](./DOC-ADDITIONAL-EVIDENCE.md) contributes other scoped realizations, without changing the core or supplying that universal derivation.
