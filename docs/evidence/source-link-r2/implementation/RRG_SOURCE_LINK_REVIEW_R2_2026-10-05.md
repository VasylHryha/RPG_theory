## 2. Main recommendation

Keep the latest theory direction. Improve the connections between a claim, a particular physical operation, and its source—not the number of references. Archiving a predecessor document does not invalidate the external papers it cited. Recover useful evidence into the current catalogue while retaining the archived document as history.

The central source-direction question remains `B_n → R_n → B_(n+1) → R_(n+1)` with `R=(G,M)` and geometry–mode reciprocity. Prioritize sources that connect two or more relevant operations inside the same described system. Keep simpler examples as explanations and mathematical reductions as methods; neither is a reason to narrow the entire theory.

Preserve the existing two independent labels: result type (experiment/simulation/theory) and relevance (component/partial bridge/analogy/constraint). Specify the exact supported subclaim and direction of influence alongside those labels; a broad H label is not itself a support statement. “Feature” below means editorial usefulness for the latest question, not a probability of truth or evidence that a paper tested RRG by name.

## 3. Recommended featured reading

**Optical supramolecules (He 2019).** Restore as a leading local physical bridge. The paper describes pulse-driven acoustic feedback, competing dispersive-wave repulsion, and nested assemblies containing different solitonic units. Its Results also report noisy spikes becoming solitons after pump changes and incorporation into an existing assembly. These findings deserve positive credit, not merely an “apparatus was supplied” dismissal. Keep cavity/pump assumptions and distinguish finite hierarchy from indefinite recursion or the origin of fundamental matter. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-13746-6).

**Liposome feedback (Fu 2023).** Restore as a geometry–activity feedback case in a supplied ATP-driven system. Separate experimental motion/protein-pattern findings from the reduced model: its motion coupling is phenomenological and the simulation includes a manually imposed initial symmetry-breaking displacement. Do not turn a modeled feedback control into an experimentally performed ablation. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-023-02058-8).

**Environmental pH (Ratzke & Gore 2018).** Add or recover into the active catalogue: organisms change an environment that changes growth conditions. Facilitation and harmful outcomes both matter. This links naturally to the life/environment question without claiming a newly created species or a measured universal resonance mechanism. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.2004248).

Keep current E19 for random-field interactions and E07 for atom–cavity feedback. E08 also reports synchronization-dependent lateral forces; this is useful effective-interaction context beyond generic geometry/phase coupling. E20 includes band-dependent changes in flow as well as multiplication, annihilation and parameter regimes without bands. Show those positive feedback and limiting findings together. Reintroduce Weng for heterogeneous building blocks and the Prindle/Liu studies for communication and coordination between existing communities. Do not make replication compulsory across the framework.

## 7. Constraints and methods that add value

E17 should remain beside E09: they prevent a conditional resonance-selection example from becoming an unconditional maximum-absorption rule. E22 remains a nonresonant comparator. Stationary Turing patterns and packing are useful comparison cases, not automatic contradictions to the project’s broad mode definition.

BIB-0007, Muljarov’s [Rigorous Theory of Coupled Resonators](https://doi.org/10.1103/fqzx-xtl9), should be tagged **methods plus constraint**. Its original abstract both challenges familiar approximations for open resonators and supplies a more rigorous treatment. A generic “supports coupled resonators” summary loses this important distinction.

Keep Mori, controlled amplitude approximations, Kron reduction, coarse-grained Langevin methods, effective field theory and RG in a technical reading path. They help construct descriptions and models. They do not by themselves demonstrate the physical formation of a new environment. Spectral-action and emergent-gauge proposals stay explicitly related theoretical frameworks; they do not redefine the RRG core.

Keep physical-neural-network and neural-manifold sources in the AI/learning branch, rather than asking those papers to prove RRG improves learning. No RRG-specific benchmark gain follows from the fact that a physical system can compute.

## 11. Minimal link contract for local implementation

Use the existing reference/citation structures wherever possible. Each displayed research link should have a narrow positive statement of what the paper contributes, the relevant current claim/case, the supplied conditions, the important limitation, its result type and its actual read depth. “Does not prove all of RRG” alone is not an informative support description.

For example: “A pulse-generated acoustic field changes pulse interactions and supports larger optical assemblies in the supplied fibre-laser system” is more useful than “supports resonance.”

Preserve the distinction between a **publication** (normally identified by DOI), a **study/experiment or dataset**, the project’s **case interpretation**, and a **document-and-edition-qualified local label**. A DOI is not a guaranteed independent-study identifier. Several papers can share data; one paper can contain several experiments. Author manuscripts, publisher copies and PubMed records can represent versions or access paths for one publication lineage. Do not merge scientific findings across versions without checking what each version actually contains. The existing reference/primaryId machinery can represent verified alternate links; no wholesale BIB renumbering is needed.

Local-label keys must include `(source document, edition, local label)`. Edition alone is insufficient: current 04 §39 uses `C1` for possibility-space growth, while current 06 uses `C1` for geometry–mode reciprocity. Bibliographic aliases and claim-label aliases are different mappings; do not route claim symbols through a DOI alias slot.
