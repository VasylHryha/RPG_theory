# Start with RRG

Recursive Resonant Geometry asks how arrangement and activity support a
persistent whole, and how that whole can help make another organization possible.
A cell maintaining its boundary and bacteria changing the acidity around them
make two parts of the question concrete.

Read [the public introduction](https://vasylhryha.github.io/rrg_theory/start/),
then choose [an example](https://vasylhryha.github.io/rrg_theory/examples/),
[evidence](https://vasylhryha.github.io/rrg_theory/evidence/) or
[the original documents](https://vasylhryha.github.io/rrg_theory/documents/).
RRG is a research proposal; local findings and wider open claims have different
status. The contact is **vasylhryha.rrg@gmail.com**.

For the exact current scientific edition, read
[source authority](research/RRG_CURRENT/SOURCE_AUTHORITY.md) and
[the manifest](research/RRG_CURRENT/CURRENT_MANIFEST.md).
Original files and explanatory website exports are distinct.
