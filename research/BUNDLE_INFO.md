# Installed research inputs

`RRG_CURRENT/` is the sole operative source copy, extracted from the actual
owner-supplied `RRG_CURRENT.zip` on 1 October 2026. Its file inventory and raw
hashes are recorded in `config/research-source.json`.

`history/` holds fourteen unchanged historical files from the R4 handoff.
`project-notes/` holds two unchanged note transcriptions with their original
Project file IDs. Their identities are in `source-manifest.json`. Neither history
nor a notice substitutes for a current scientific document.

`publication/` contains new draft website explanations, not copied framework or
core documents. Current sources may be explicitly revised through the plan's
recorded revision process; builds and intake never change them implicitly.

No research source, original ZIP, administrative plan or evidence working folder
is a public website asset. Public distribution and rights remain undecided.
