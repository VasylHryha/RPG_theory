## A star changes what can happen

Gas gathers into a star. Its heat and pressure allow nuclear reactions that were unavailable in the earlier setting. The star changes the conditions for what can form next. [See the star example](/examples/star/).

RRG calls the surrounding activity and conditions a [background](/glossary/#background). It includes available material, energy exchange, boundaries and interactions, as well as activity too detailed to follow individually. At the proposed beginning, it is fluctuating activity without a lasting organizing rhythm standing out. At later levels, it already contains structures and their history.

## From background to structure to changed background

In RRG’s proposal, temporary arrangements appear within that setting. An arrangement whose shape and activity support each other can last as a [resonator](/glossary/#resonator). It then becomes part of the conditions experienced by other things.

The shorthand is **Bₙ → Rₙ → Bₙ₊₁**. B means background; R means resonator; n marks the level being considered. Read it as the current setting, a lasting organization, then the setting inherited by the next step. Earlier structures and their activity continue underneath.

A lasting thing may become a [building block](/glossary/#building-block) in a larger whole. Separately, it may create [changed surroundings](/glossary/#changed-surroundings). This page follows the second role. Changing conditions can make similar things more likely, or make new things possible or impossible.

## A landscape of valleys

Picture a landscape whose valleys represent possible lasting combinations of shape and activity. The surroundings determine which valleys exist, how deep they are and which paths can reach them. A path represents a route by which an organization could form.

When conditions change, a previously unreachable valley may become accessible. Another may disappear. This is why the question is what can form and last under the conditions now available. The next structure need not already exist as a finished object. Opening one route can close another.

## Changing the world and changing our description

A star physically changes temperature, pressure and composition. Those effects occur whether anyone describes them.

We can also describe a complicated system using fewer details, treating a lasting whole as one useful unit. That changes our description. It does not itself create a new physical structure. A proposed next-level object must actually form and persist; giving it a simpler description is insufficient.

## Status and further reading

The locked core calls background and possibility-space change an **extension compatible with the core**. It is outside the minimal definition. The effect depends on interactions, energy and available material; another level is possible, not inevitable.
