Everything moves. Most motion is chaos. Sometimes a piece of motion falls into a rhythm that keeps its own shape, and that shape keeps the rhythm going. It lasts: that is a resonator. Its rhythm reaches out. It makes nearby things move with it, stick to it, or take a similar shape. So more resonators appear, they join into bigger shapes with a new rhythm of their own, and the same thing happens again at the next level.

[RRG is a proposed framework, not a proven law.](#what-is-established-and-what-is-rrgs-proposal)

## The name is the rules

Order appears by chance: most shapes vanish; some hold.

### Resonant → resonance

Motion falls into rhythm.

### Geometry → shape

Rhythm and shape hold each other, so it lasts.

### Recursive → resonator

It spreads and joins into bigger shapes with a new resonance; the same rules repeat at each new scale.

**Rhythm ⇄ shape → resonator → bigger shape → repeat.**

Rhythm and shape hold each other; neither comes first.

## Two ways to open the next step

### Becomes a building block

Atoms join into [molecules](/examples/molecule/).

### Changes its surroundings

A [star](/examples/star/)’s heat and pressure allow new elements; changed surroundings can make similar things more likely, or new things impossible.

## The ladder

What science knows is the history; reading it as one repeating step is RRG's proposal.

### Matter and cosmos

#### Hot quantum fields / quark–gluon plasma

Quarks and gluons fill the early universe.

**Changes surroundings**

#### Protons and neutrons

Cooling binds quarks into protons and neutrons.

**Building block**

#### Light nuclei

Protons and neutrons form light nuclei.

**Building block**

#### [Neutral atoms and the first molecule](/examples/molecule/)

Cooling allows atoms and helium hydride (HeH⁺).

**Building block**

#### [Stars](/examples/star/)

Gas gathers into stars.

**Changes surroundings**

#### Heavy elements

Stars forge carbon and scatter heavier elements.

**Changes surroundings**

#### [Planets and rich chemistry](/examples/life-environment/)

These elements build planets and richer chemistry.

**Changes surroundings**

### The route to life

#### Prebiotic networks

Chemistry forms networks of reactions.

**Building block**

### Life and mind

#### [Cells](/examples/cell/)

Cells exchange matter and energy.

**Both ways**

#### Multicellular organisms

Cells join into bodies.

**Building block**

#### Nervous systems

Connected cells coordinate activity.

**Building block**

#### Humans, technology / AI

Humans create tools and AI.

**Changes surroundings**

### Same carbon, different arrangement

The same carbon atoms, [arranged differently](/concepts/geometry-and-modes/), make different materials.

## One carbon atom’s journey

**Forged in a star → molecules → cells → us.**

Stars forge and scatter carbon. One atom can join molecules, cells and us.

## Life is a late part of the same ladder

[Cells](/examples/cell/) maintain themselves; [bacteria can change acidity](/references/#BIB-0089) around them.

Life survives first; successful life then shapes its surroundings for itself and spreads. Joining also helps: cells form bodies and organisms participate in ecosystems, while simpler levels continue underneath. No intention is needed: what holds and spreads becomes more common.

Humans reshape the planet with tools and cities; AI may begin another cycle.

Did life invent this logic, or is it older?

## Look closer

### What holds things together?

Forces gather and hold; resonance decides what fits and lasts. In RRG, “decides” means physical compatibility, not choice. The strong force dominates nuclear binding; electromagnetism supports atoms, chemistry and life; gravity gathers stars, planets and galaxies. These are dominant roles, not one force per scale; the weak force also matters in transformations and decay. [Read about forces and fit](/documents/world-explanation/#61-forces-gather-and-hold-resonance-decides-what-fits-and-lasts).

In RRG’s picture, inner resonance means compatible activity within a thing; outer resonance means compatible coupling to others. Neither adds a force or requires identical frequencies. New levels bring new ways to interact, such as chemical bonds between atoms. [Read about new interactions](/documents/world-explanation/#73-new-levels-bring-new-ways-to-interact) or [explore interactions](/concepts/effective-interactions/).

Could some forces be resonance, or emerge as new levels form? [See what remains open](#what-is-established-and-what-is-rrgs-proposal).

### Energy and time

Everything moves, and energy is what keeps things moving: vibrating, rotating, resonating. Every resonator lasts for a time. Some hold on their own for billions of years; others last only while energy keeps flowing through them.

This is broad wording: energy is not just visible motion. Passive bound structures can persist without continuous outside power; actively maintained systems need energy flow. [Read about energy and time](/documents/world-explanation/#31-energy-and-time).

Each level needs relevant earlier material and conditions: hydrogen and helium → earlier stars forge and scatter elements → our Sun and planets form from that richer material → water and steady sunlight → conditions for life as we know it. This is not a compulsory path through every possible level. [Read about earlier conditions](/documents/world-explanation/#72-each-level-needs-the-ones-before-it) or [see the stellar step](/examples/star/).

### Key words

- **Geometry:** the parts, their shape, connections and constraints. [Geometry](/concepts/geometry-and-modes/).
- **Resonance:** motion that has fallen into rhythm. [Resonance](/concepts/geometry-and-modes/).
- **Resonator:** a whole whose shape and activity support each other. [Resonators](/concepts/geometry-and-modes/).
- **Stability:** shape and activity support each other for a time; permanence is not promised. [Stability](/concepts/stability/).
- **Scale:** a level where a whole acts as a useful unit. [Scale](/concepts/recursion/).
- **Recursion:** lasting wholes can become parts of larger wholes, and the cycle repeats; their different parts stay active. [Recursion](/concepts/recursion/).

### What is established, and what is RRG’s proposal

The cosmic history is established; reading it as one repeating step is RRG’s proposal. The historical route from prebiotic chemistry to first life remains unresolved. The acidity experiment concerns already living bacteria. The drawings are schematics, not measured sizes or literal quantum shapes.

Spreading is a strong tendency, not guaranteed, in RRG’s proposal: it needs suitable interactions, surroundings and energy. Repeated appearances alone do not prove causal spreading. Reproduction is not required. Becoming a building block is the core idea; changing surroundings is a newer part of RRG. [Read the core](/documents/locked-core/) and [the background explanation](/framework/recursive-background/).

The forces themselves are established. RRG’s claim that resonance decides what fits, and whether some forces are themselves resonance, remain open. The suggestion that forces appeared over time through earlier levels is speculative and may never be directly confirmed. New ways to interact do not demonstrate new fundamental forces. The energy wording is RRG’s broad framing. Its account of evolution, and AI as a new cycle, are proposed readings. [Read the established/proposed boundary](/documents/world-explanation/#15-what-this-explanation-is-not-saying).

RRG can be checked where we can reach: experiments show rhythms shaping matter, synchronizing and multiplying patterns. A stronger test would show the full step twice in one system: one lasting organization changes its surroundings so a different lasting organization can form, then that step happens again. The current catalogue has not shown that complete repeated construction. The very beginning and origin of forces may remain beyond direct evidence. [Read how RRG can be checked](/documents/world-explanation/#151-where-rrg-can-be-checked) or [check the evidence](/evidence/).

### What RRG does not claim

RRG does not claim to explain fundamental forces, cosmology or the origin of life, replace current physics, or give every object one special frequency. [Read the limits](/documents/world-explanation/#15-what-this-explanation-is-not-saying) or [browse common questions](/documents/world-explanation/#16-common-questions).
