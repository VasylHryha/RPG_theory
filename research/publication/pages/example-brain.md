## What happens

A brain is a physical network. Its connections constrain which patterns of activity can occur. Activity changes from moment to moment, while learning changes the wiring and the effective relationships between its parts. The network shapes what can happen, and what happens can reshape the network.

Neuroscience also studies coordinated activity across populations of neurons. One approach describes that activity within a **neural manifold**: a constrained space of possible collective states. Each state describes what the population is doing together at a moment. A trajectory follows how that collective activity changes over time.

## How RRG reads it

RRG calls the connections and constraints [geometry](/glossary/#geometry-shape). The changing patterns of activity belong to its [mode structure](/glossary/#mode), which includes timing, relationships and response. It need not be one repeating beat.

**Geometry** concerns the network’s arrangement; **Resonant** concerns its coordinated activity. RRG reads their relationship in both directions: wiring shapes activity, and activity helps change the relationships that shape future activity. Its term [inner resonance](/glossary/#inner-resonance) names compatible activity within an organization.

In this intuitive picture, a thought can be imagined as a trajectory through changing internal relationships. Learning reshapes those relationships and the paths future activity can follow.

## What you can see here

The clearest feature is **shape and activity affecting each other**. Learning changes which future patterns are easier or harder to follow. A recognizable organization can persist while its internal relationships change.

The main focus is internal organization. Perception and action connect it to the world: sensory interactions shape internal activity, and actions change the outside world. RRG reads this as an internal organization modelling useful relationships in its surroundings. Becoming a building block or enabling another lasting organization would be a further question.

## Where the interpretation stops

The trajectory picture does not explain consciousness or establish what a thought feels like. The neural-manifold approach is related scientific work; it does not demonstrate RRG’s common cycle across scales.
