A string shows which motions fit a shape. Water lets you follow a moving pattern. Then the ladder follows first lasting structures, molecules, carbon arrangements, stars, cells, life changing its surroundings and the brain. This is a reading order; the carbon example compares arrangements along the way.

Each example follows the same questions: what happens, how RRG reads it, which rules and outward roles you can see, and where that reading stops. The mechanisms differ; the shared pattern is RRG’s proposal.
