Choose the question you want to follow. Each example explains an actual situation, the physical relationships that matter and the particular RRG connection it illustrates. They use different mechanisms.

## Continue the introduction

- [Cell: organization through exchange](/examples/cell/) — follow a boundary, transport and ongoing maintenance.
- [Life changes its environment](/examples/life-environment/) — see how bacterial pH changes can help or hinder another population, then consider the broader oxygen example.

## Compare another physical setting

- [Water: follow a pattern](/examples/water/) — follow the moving crest separately from the water carrying it.
- [String: boundaries shape motion](/examples/string/) — compare supported patterns under fixed ends, tension and material conditions.
- [Star: new reaction conditions](/examples/star/) — follow existing organization into conditions for later transformations.

The wave and string introduce particular relationships; the cell and culture examples develop feedback. This ordering is a reading path, not a ranking of scientific evidence or a history of inevitable levels.
