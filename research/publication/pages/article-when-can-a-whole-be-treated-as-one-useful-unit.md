## Start with an intact molecule

A molecule contains atoms, electron states and collective motion. Yet another chemical description can treat it as one participant. We have not erased its parts; we have chosen the properties relevant to a particular interaction. The [molecule example](/examples/molecule/) traces this back to the supplied world explanation.

Now change the question. If the molecule dissociates, continuing to count one intact participant hides a material change. A useful unit is therefore not simply whatever we can draw a boundary around. Its usefulness depends on the circumstances and the response we need to describe.

## What can remain unresolved?

RRG asks which collective organization persists while its parts remain active. A later description may track an intact whole's motion, relationships or response without tracking every lower-level detail. If those hidden details affect the answer, the description needs to retain more of them.

This does not mean that all useful reductions are simple averages. The current [background companion](/framework/recursive-background/) explains that correlations, mode patterns, coupling and memory may matter. Reducing everything to one number can discard the very relation that made a whole useful.

## Organization and activity belong together

The registered RRG object is :claim[UT-D03]. Its geometry includes relationships, boundaries and constraints. Its mode structure includes phases, coupling, responses and characteristic times, beyond one frequency.

The [stability definition](/claims/UT-D04/) concerns self-consistent geometry–mode organization over an interval. It is not permanence, and it does not prescribe one lifetime threshold for molecules, cells and every other system.

The [scale definition](/claims/UT-D05/) asks when a collective organization functions as an effective unit in further interactions. Parts can differ and remain internally active. The [recursion definition](/claims/UT-D08/) permits further combination without requiring an identical microscopic mechanism or compulsory copying at every step.

## A mathematical question with a stated scope

The [candidate mathematics](/math/) compares two routes: evolve the detailed system and then reduce it, or reduce it and predict the collective evolution. A useful comparison needs stated variables, maps, conditions and an error measure.

That is a model-specific research test. It does not establish that a new physical level formed, or require every scale to obey the same equation family. The stronger same-family question remains optional. No such calculation is executed in this article.

## The whole has another possible role

A cell may be one participant in a larger organization while continuing its internal exchanges. Its activity may also change its surroundings. Treating the cell as a unit and measuring that environmental change answer different questions.

[How existing structures make new organization possible](/articles/how-existing-structures-make-new-organization-possible/) follows the second role. Read the two together without assuming that success at one operation proves the complete recursive proposal.

The question to take into a concrete model is: **for this interaction and interval, which collective properties suffice, and what would require us to describe the parts again?** [Explore recursion and scale](/concepts/recursion/) for the shorter definition-led path.
