## Questions worth keeping open

If a vibrating plate makes a pattern, what does that tell us about a cell, a star or gravity? The jump between those examples is exactly where careful questions help. Familiar effects can support individual connections without establishing a common explanation.

**RRG is a hypothesis:** shape and activity can support a lasting organization, which may spread and change what can form next. The questions below separate that proposal from the things already demonstrated. A useful objection identifies a claim and the observation that would challenge it.

## Isn't this just self-organization or emergence?

Much of the starting ground is shared. Existing theories already explain many effects in the catalogue. RRG proposes to join formation, spreading and change of the [background](/glossary/#background)—the surrounding material, activity and conditions—into one repeated cycle. Its proposed rule for what connects and lasts is [resonance-fit](/glossary/#resonance-fit): forces gather and hold, while the fit of shape and activity is proposed to explain which arrangements connect and last. There is no distinguishing evidence for that addition yet. The [comparison with existing work](/framework/#17-relation-to-existing-work) explains the overlap and proposed differences; calling a known effect “RRG” does not give it a new explanation.

## Can anything disprove it?

The risk is that words such as “organization” and “resonance” become broad enough to fit every outcome. A testable version needs a defined system, measurable conditions and an outcome it excludes. If it predicts that a formed structure changes the background to enable a new kind of lasting arrangement, a controlled result showing no such change challenges that version. A counterexample within the stated domain of a precise universal claim challenges that claim. Failure of one toy model addresses that model. Conditional claims do not promise every system will spread. See [how failures should change the project](/framework/recursive-background/#37-how-objections-and-failures-should-change-the-project).

## What about gravity?

Gravity is the hardest open case. RRG has not derived it, and does not equate a force with a frequency. Its proposed [inner fit](/glossary/#inner-resonance), within an organization, and [outer fit](/glossary/#outer-resonance), between organizations and their surroundings, need defined observations and predictions. Coupled oscillators and optical-field cases do not explain gravitational attraction merely because they connect objects. The [gravity discussion](/framework/#12-gravity-hardest-open-physics-problem-for-rrg) and [open-claim registry](/research-status/claims/#open-claim-registry) keep that gap visible.

## Does it predict anything new?

No distinctive quantitative prediction is established by the current evidence. The reachable direction is to show a persistent organization changing its background so another kind of lasting arrangement can form, then repeat the operation in one system. A stronger test would compare a numerical prediction with other explanations, without inserting the answer while building the model. Familiar successes alone cannot decide between explanations. Read [what would count as strong evidence](/framework/recursive-background/#35-what-would-count-as-strong-evidence-for-rrg).

## Why publish before proof?

Sharing a clear, labelled proposal lets others inspect the connections, supply cases, correct citations and offer counterexamples. The evidence catalogue states what was observed, what was supplied and where interpretation begins. Publication is an invitation to discussion; it does not substitute for the missing derivations or independent experiments. Changes and unresolved findings should remain visible in the history. The [reading guide](/documents/reading-guide/#a-clear-public-evidence-format) describes that practice.

## Is it a theory of everything?

It is a unifying framework, not a completed account of all physics. The ambition reaches across scales, but the shared rule, fundamental interactions and cosmological connection remain open. Mathematical reduction is also different from physically creating a new level. Complexity may stop, branch or break down; it is not guaranteed to rise forever. The [plain explanation's limits](/documents/world-explanation/#15-what-this-explanation-is-not-saying) separate the ambition from what has been shown.
